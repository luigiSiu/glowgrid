## If you shoot more

Photograph the panel **dim**. LEDs at anything above brightness 10 blow out to
white blobs on camera and the shape of the icon disappears entirely, even though
it looks fine to the eye. Some ambient light helps too — a pitch-dark room gives
the camera nothing to expose for except the LEDs.
